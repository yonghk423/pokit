## 공통 지침

Use case: illustration-story. Create ONE standalone LANDSCAPE 3:2 illustration for a daily-life app.
REFERENCE: Match the attached three examples closely in drawing style, simplicity and detail level: graceful adult East Asian woman, black ink manga linework, black hair and clothing masses, mostly white space, a little understated screentone only. Keep the SAME restrained drawn look. Avoid dense textures, photorealistic interiors, elaborate backgrounds, glamour or fashion shoots. The background consists of just the few things needed to recognize the activity.
COMPOSITION: a candid third-person view of an ordinary moment, like the reference woman reading and the reference woman at her computer. She is absorbed in the activity and unaware of the observer. Her gaze follows the task. No camera eye contact, no deliberate portrait pose, no beauty-shot presentation. Show hands and useful activity context. Moderate natural stylized proportions.
EXPRESSION: subtle and believable for the specific activity. Lips usually closed and relaxed; no automatic grin, no compulsory smile, no cute blushing, no eyes squeezed shut for a photo. A small spontaneous pleased expression is appropriate only where specified.
OUTPUT: black-and-white illustration only, no color, no headings, lettering, UI, border, watermark or collage.
