Daylight Pro subscription review

No app account or sign-in is required. An internet connection is required to load subscription products, complete purchases, restore purchases, and download Pro content.

How to access the subscription screen:
1. Open the app and complete the introductory screens if shown.
2. Open Settings from the main screen.
3. Tap "Start Pro" to open the Pro screen.
4. Select Monthly or Yearly, then tap "Subscribe" to open the Apple purchase sheet.

Products:
- monthly: Daylight Pro Monthly, auto-renewing, 1 month.
- yearly: Daylight Pro Yearly, auto-renewing, 1 year.
Both plans provide the same Daylight Pro benefits; only the billing period differs. Pro includes premium illustration calendar packs and Pro film filters. The app also includes free calendar designs and free filters.

Restore: Tap "Restore purchases" at the bottom of the Pro screen, or use Restore purchases in Settings.
Manage or change plan: For an active subscriber, open the Pro screen and tap "Manage subscription / Change plan" to open Apple subscription management.

The Pro screen displays the localized App Store price and billing period. Terms of Use and Privacy Policy links are available at the bottom of that screen.

Photo library and calendar permissions are only needed for the related optional features; they are not required to open the subscription screen.
